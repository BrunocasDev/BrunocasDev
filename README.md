## What I do

Five years building for real businesses — car dealerships, boat charters, a
historic bookshop, an accountancy firm. The brief is usually the same: the
current site does not bring in work, or the team is losing hours a week to
spreadsheets.

- **Business websites** that get found and turn visitors into enquiries
- **Booking platforms and e-commerce** with real availability and payments
- **Back-office systems** built around an existing process, not against it
- **Technical SEO, performance and accessibility** — treated as part of the job

Currently a consultant at **Affinity**, assigned to **LeYa**. I develop the
Lyvros e-commerce platform, and built and ran the Livraria Buchholz platform
single-handedly, from first commit to production.
